Registration

A .mesh domain is mapped to the endpoint selected by the owner. To register a .mesh domain it has to be mined, which is the process of making a computer guess numbers until one with a difficulty of at least 31 bits is found (31 bits is about 2.147 billion attempts). If it is already registered, you will have to beat the difficulty set by the current owner

Seed

The seed acts as a unique password with which the endpoint can be changed without re-mining the domain. A seed of at least 10 characters with special characters is advised
